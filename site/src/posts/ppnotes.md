PP = Personal & Private

PPnotes is an iOS native voice to note application. User can quickly speak and PPnotes use Apple On Device LLM to transcribe and manage the notes. Everything on iPhone, no cloud.

<img src="/assets/imported/ppnotes-figure-01.jpg" style="color:transparent;object-fit:contain;object-position:center;height:auto" loading="lazy" decoding="async" data-nimg="1" width="710" height="399" alt="image" />

PPnotes is perfect for people to have flash ideas every moment. And once noted, you can free your mind.

Features:

1.  push-to-talk style recording, helps user to focus on thoughts rather than the screen
2.  automation note categorization, powered by Apple On Device LLM
3.  follow-ups: integration to To-do, Calendar, Reminders, Email (quick ones), etc.
