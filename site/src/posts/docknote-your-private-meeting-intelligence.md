**Now in early access alpha test**

> Audio stays on your Mac. Transcribed to text, on your Mac. The cloud computes on text, then wipes. Everything kept on your Mac.

**How it works**

## Audio stays. Only text travels.

Audio is transcribed on your Mac; only text crosses to SnapGPU, which summarizes and erases it.

🔒

**LOCAL TRANSCRIPTION**

**Your words never leave your Mac.**

Recordings are transcribed right on your machine. The audio is never uploaded — not to transcribe it, not after.

☁️

**SNAPGPU**

**Text only, and only for a moment.**

To summarize, just the text goes to a cloud GPU that works in memory, hands the result back, then erases everything.

**What you get**

## Three things, done well.

📝

**A clean transcript**

Every recording, with who spoke what and when.

📋

**A short summary**

The gist, the decisions, the next steps.

💾

**Yours to keep**

Everything stays on your device.

**A note on accuracy.** Keeping your audio private means it's transcribed by on-device models, which can trail the largest cloud models — so transcripts and summaries may contain errors. Please double-check anything that matters.

🛡️

**Your conversations stay yours.** Audio stays on your Mac. Only text crosses to the cloud — and only for the moment it takes to summarize.

## Honest, because it's an alpha

Some recordings transcribe more cleanly than others, and a summary is a helpful draft — not the final word. The AI never runs on its own; it does something only when you ask. If anything feels off, tell us.
