# Owner brief

## User and job

Readers need a fast, stable home for Sean's long-form engineering writing. Sean needs a publishing system that costs nothing to host, does not depend on Notion rendering, and keeps content in Git as portable Markdown.

## Outcome

- A distinctive seanslab homepage and readable long-form article layout.
- Existing public article URLs preserved.
- Fifteen public pieces and their illustrations owned locally.
- Automatic GitHub deployments and branch previews.
- SEO essentials: canonical URLs, Open Graph/Twitter metadata, Atom feed, sitemap, robots, and Google verification.
- No production JavaScript, database, server process, or recurring vendor fee.

## Non-goals for v1

- CMS editing UI, comments, accounts, search, analytics, newsletter automation, and automatic Notion sync.
- Publishing every draft and Chinese/social-media variant in the flat source folder.
- Changing the domain registrar or nameservers.
